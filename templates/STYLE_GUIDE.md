## The user's own rules (these win)

## In one paragraph
What this style is, who it is for, and what makes it recognisable.

## Structure and pacing
Typical length, how it opens (hook), how it moves (shots per minute, words per minute), how it ends.

## Hook
What happens in the first 3 seconds: text, framing, first words.

## Captions
Font, weight, size, colour, outline/box, case, words per line, where on screen, how each line appears,
whether the spoken word is highlighted.

## Titles and on-screen text
When text other than captions appears (on numbers, lists, key claims), what it looks like, how long it holds.

## Shots, framing and punch-ins
Framing of the speaker, how often the shot changes, push-ins, b-roll share.

## Colour
The look in words (warm/cool, contrast, saturation) and the measured numbers from scan/colour.json.

## Audio and music
Music under the voice or not, how loud, fades, sound effects.

## What the kit cannot copy automatically
Anything in the references the editor does not do by itself (animated graphics, stickers, green-screen, 3D
transitions): describe each so it can be added by hand in Resolve.

Things this style never does.
